The visual language of BOMHort — logo, colors, typography, and components. Use these tokens and assets to keep the brand consistent across the website, docs, product UI, and community materials.

Meet the BOMHort dragon — a friendly guardian hoarding a stack of SBOM documents. Always keep clear space around it and never distort, recolor, or add effects.

Typography

Typeface: Inter, with a system-font fallback stack. Headings are tight and bold; body copy is comfortable at 1.6 line-height.
